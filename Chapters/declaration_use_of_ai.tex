\section{Declaration on the Use of Generative AI}
\label{sec:declaration_use_of_ai}

Generative AI systems and AI-based tools were used during the preparation of this thesis
in a supporting role only. In the implementation, GitHub Copilot and Claude Code were
used for code suggestions and development support. In the writing, AI tools were used to
assist with literature and source discovery, to explain technical concepts, for
translation assistance, and for language editing of text written by the authors,
including proofreading and improvements to wording. No part of the thesis text was
generated wholesale by an AI system, and no experimental result, analysis or
interpretation was produced by one: every number reported here comes from the authors'
own experiments, and every claim was formulated and verified by the authors. All output
produced with the support of these tools was reviewed, validated and adapted by the
authors, who remain fully responsible for the correctness, originality and integrity of
the presented work.
